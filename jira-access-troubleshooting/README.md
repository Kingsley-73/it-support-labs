# Jira Service Management: Employee Access Troubleshooting

## Project Overview

This project documents a simulated IT support ticket investigating an employee's inability to access a Finance application following a password reset.

**Environment:** Jira Service Management  
**Ticket ID:** SUP-1  
**Project Type:** Simulated IT Support Lab  
**Focus Areas:** Ticket management, troubleshooting, access control, documentation, and escalation.

> **Important:** This is a fictional training scenario. No real employee accounts, production systems, or application permissions were changed.

## Scenario

A fictional Finance Assistant, Alex Morgan, reports that access to the Finance application is denied after a password reset.

Initial triage establishes the following:

- The employee can sign in to their company account.
- Another employee can access the Finance application.
- The issue affects one employee.
- The employee's application role is `Employee — Basic`.
- The required role for the employee's duties is `Finance Assistant`.
- A fictional approved access request, `AR-1042`, is available for verification.

## Investigation

The findings suggest an application-specific access or role-assignment issue rather than a general company-account sign-in failure.

The documented investigation and planned actions were:

1. Review the reported symptoms and establish the scope of the issue.
2. Compare the employee's current application role with the role required for their duties.
3. Verify the approved access request before authorizing any permission change.
4. Refer the role correction to an authorized administrator.
5. Ask the employee to retest access after the change.
6. Document the outcome and validation evidence.

## Simulated Resolution

For this training scenario, the approved request was treated as verified, an authorized administrator's role correction was assumed, and the employee's successful retest was simulated.

The intended result was access consistent with the employee's approved job requirements, without granting unnecessary privileges.

**Actual system change:** None.  
**Validation:** Simulated successful retest.  
**Jira workflow status:** The transition to Resolved could not be completed because Jira returned an invalid-resolution configuration error. The ticket was not confirmed as resolved in Jira.

## Skills Demonstrated

- Creating and assigning a support ticket
- Recording investigation notes
- Scoping and triaging an access issue
- Identifying an application-role mismatch
- Recognizing the need to verify authorization
- Escalating permission changes appropriately
- Planning post-change validation
- Documenting findings, limitations, and outcomes

## Learning Outcome

This exercise demonstrates a structured approach to access-related support requests: establish the facts, investigate the likely cause, verify authorization, involve the appropriate administrator, validate the outcome, and document the work accurately.

The scenario is simulated and does not establish experience administering a live identity platform.
